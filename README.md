A tiny NFC card with one purpose: **make it easy to find my GitHub.**

Just. Scan the card with an NFC-enabled phone and the card takes you straight to my GitHub profile, where you can check out my repositories, projects and the things I have been building.

##  What's Inside?

*  NFC antenna

*  LED indicator

* 🔩 Resistor

* ⚡ Capacitor

* 🪪 NFC card PCB

Nothing complicated. Just a small fun hardware project that combines electronics with my love for building things.

# How It Works

When you bring your phone close to the card the NFC tag is. Opens my GitHub profile.

**Tap → Scan → GitHub → Explore my projects.**

That is basically it. 😄

##  Why I Made It

I wanted to make something that represents what I enjoy doing. **Electronics, hardware and coding**.

Of handing someone a username or asking them to search for me I can just hand them the card and let the NFC do the work.
